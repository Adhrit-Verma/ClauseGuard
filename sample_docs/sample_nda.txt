MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

1. Confidentiality. Each party agrees to hold the other party's Confidential
Information in strict confidence, in perpetuity, and to use it solely for
the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship.

2. Term and Auto-Renewal. This Agreement shall remain in effect for an
initial term of one (1) year and shall automatically renew for successive
one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal
at least five (5) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3. Limitation of Liability. In no event shall Disclosing Party's total
liability arising out of this Agreement exceed one hundred dollars ($100),
regardless of the form of action.

4. Indemnification. Receiving Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold
harmless Disclosing Party from any and all claims, losses, and damages of
any kind arising from Receiving Party's breach of this Agreement, without
limitation.

5. Termination. This Agreement may be terminated by either party only upon
a material breach by the other party that remains uncured for thirty (30)
days after written notice.

6. Payment Terms. Any fees owed under a subsequent definitive agreement
shall be due within ten (10) days of invoice, with a late fee of 3% per
month on overdue amounts.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
jurisdiction most favorable to the Disclosing Party, without regard to
conflict of law principles.
